Nova Models Amazon Nova Foundation Models Cost-effective, customizable, and trusted models for enterprise AI User Guide 
for Amazon Nova Build with Amazon Nova Latest news and updates on Amazon Nova About Amazon Nova Amazon Nova foundation 
models help enterprises move AI from pilot to production at scale, with confidence, and at a fraction of the cost. 
Whether you're automating document processing, analyzing multimodal media, building voice AI applications, or running 
everyday AI workloads, Nova gives you the capabilities to make production AI viable, so you don't have to choose 
between performance, cost, and trust. Play Build for Enterprise AI Cost-Effective Delivers strong performance across a 
broad range of enterprise workloads while keeping costs low and token efficiency high, enabling scalable deployment. 
Customizable Shape intelligence that understands your business with the most comprehensive suite of customization 
capabilities for any proprietary model. Enterprise-Grade Trust Built with principles of Amazon's Frontier Model Safety 
Framework, with uncapped indemnity and comprehensive content moderation controls. Meet Nova 2 models Models designed to 
power your diverse AI use cases Nova accelerates AI innovation across modalities and capabilities, including text, 
image, video, speech, and agentic AI Nova 2 Lite Nova 2.5 Sonic Nova Multimodal Embeddings Nova 2 Lite Amazon Nova 2 
Lite is a fast, cost-effective reasoning model for most everyday tasks. It offers developer controls for thinking 
effort, built-in tools such as code interpreter and web grounding, remote MCP tool support, and a 1M-token context 
window. You can use the model for everyday AI workloads such as customer service chatbots, document processing, and 
business automation. Nova 2 Lite is also the best model to use with Nova Forge to build your own frontier intelligence 
for the most complex tasks. Play Nova 2.5 Sonic Amazon Nova 2.5 Sonic is our speech-to-speech model for building voice 
agents that engage in natural, real-time conversations. With improved reasoning, instruction following, and 
tool-calling accuracy, the model helps voice agents follow multi-step instructions and use connected tools to complete 
customer tasks. Lower latency enables faster, more natural interactions. The model unifies speech understanding and 
generation, offering best-in-class streaming speech understanding and efficient dialog handling. It supports seven 
languages, polyglot voices, cross-modal interaction for seamless switching between voice and text within a session, 
asynchronous tool use, and a context window of up to 1M tokens. You can use Nova 2.5 Sonic to build voice agents for 
customer support, interactive learning, and voice-enabled personal assistants. Play Nova Multimodal Embeddings Amazon 
Nova Multimodal Embeddings is a state-of-the-art embedding model designed to power agentic RAG and semantic search. It 
is the first unified embedding model that maps diverse content types (text, documents, images, video, and audio) into a 
unified space with leading accuracy. Organizations can unlock the value of their unstructured data by replacing 
multiple specialized models with a single unified solution to power seamless search and retrieval across content types. 
Play Benefits Enable fast, responsive experiences Nova 2 models offer low latency and high throughput, making them 
ideal for applications requiring rapid responses, such as real-time text generation, web search, and interactive 
conversational agents. Optimize inference with thinking controls and built-in tools Nova 2 provides flexible thinking 
controls (on/off, low, medium, high), allowing developers to optimize for performance, cost, and speed tradeoffs. 
Built-in tools (Web Grounding and Code Interpreter) enable grounded responses and executable workflows. Customize for 
your business needs Nova 2 Lite offers the most comprehensive suite of customization capabilities for any proprietary 
model. These capabilities are available through Amazon Bedrock and Amazon SageMaker AI across the model training 
lifecycle, including pre-training, supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT). Nova 2 Lite can 
also be used to build your own frontier model through Amazon Nova Forge. Deploy custom Nova models on Amazon SageMaker 
AI Custom Nova models built using Amazon SageMaker AI can be deployed to use SageMaker Inference for optimal inference 
performance and cost. This offers flexibility over instance types, auto-scaling policies, and concurrency settings, 
enabling control over inference price-performance for full-rank customized Nova models. Customize content moderation 
settings Amazon Nova supports custom content moderation settings for customers with approved business use cases. You 
can adjust safeguards relevant to your business requirements across safety, sensitive content, fairness, and security, 
so your applications can process the content your use case requires, including cybersecurity, media analysis, and 
evidence review. In all cases, Amazon Nova enforces essential, non-configurable controls, such as controls to prevent 
harm to children and preserve privacy. Over 50,000 customers use Amazon Nova models Trellix automates threat
