Language English Login All Collections Privacy and policies Policy FAQ How your data is used to improve model performance How your data is used to improve model performance Learn how content from OpenAI’s services may be used to improve models and how to manage your available controls. Updated: 6 days ago Overview OpenAI offers settings that help you control whether eligible content can be used to improve model performance. When enabled, OpenAI may use eligible content to improve the performance and safety of its models. You can control whether new conversations and tasks are included. Before using eligible user content to improve our models, we take steps to reduce the amount of personal information in training datasets. For more information, see: Consumer privacy. For more on how we handle data, please see our Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, and Enterprise Privacy page. Services for individuals When you use our services for individuals (Free, Plus, Pro), we may use your content to train our models. You can choose whether your conversations help improve our models. To opt out, turn off Improve the model for everyone under Settings > Data controls in ChatGPT, or select Do not train on my content in our Privacy Portal. Either option is sufficient for ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks. You don’t need to do both. After you opt out, we won’t use your new conversations to improve our models. For more information, see: Data controls in ChatGPT. Even if you have opted out of training, you can still choose to provide feedback to us about your interactions with our products (for instance, by selecting thumbs up or thumbs down on a model response). If you choose to provide feedback, the entire conversation associated with that feedback may be used to train our models. OpenAI Support conversations Conversations with OpenAI Support may be used to improve OpenAI services, including our models, if Improve the model for everyone is enabled in Settings. Codex Codex has a separate Include environments setting in Codex settings for allowing training on full environments. Changing your settings in ChatGPT or the Privacy Portal does not change that setting. Temporary chats Temporary chats are not used to improve OpenAI models while they remain temporary, even if you choose personalization. They do not appear in your chat history or create or update memories. If you save a temporary chat, it becomes a regular chat and follows your account’s personalization and model-improvement settings. For more information about temporary chat, see: Temporary chat in ChatGPT. Parental controls For a linked teen account, a parent or guardian may manage whether conversations can be used to improve our models through Parental controls. Business services and managed workspaces By default, we don’t use inputs or outputs from ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT Edu, or our API to improve our models. The same applies to content in ChatGPT for Academic Researchers workspaces. API organization owners can enable data sharing. When they do, members can choose to share feedback and the current API Playground chat to help improve OpenAI models. For information about how we handle business data, see: Enterprise privacy. FAQ What if I submit feedback after opting out? For individual plans (Free, Plus, and Pro), if you submit feedback on a response—for example, by selecting thumbs up or thumbs down—we may use the entire conversation associated with that feedback to improve our models, even if you have opted out of model training. For ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and ChatGPT Edu, OpenAI does not use prompts, responses, or files to improve its models unless the organization has signed a separate agreement allowing it. Specifically, response feedback (submitting thumbs up or thumbs down) does not permit training on the associated conversation. Workspace admins can enable or disable response feedback by navigating to Workspace settings > Allow response feedback in the Admin Console. Need more help? Language English Login All Collections ChatGPT Temporary chat in ChatGPT Temporary chat in ChatGPT Learn how temporary chat handles personalization, chat history, model improvement, and retention, and what changes when you save a chat. Updated: 6 days ago Overview A temporary chat stays out of your chat history and is not used to improve OpenAI models while it remains temporary. It can use existing memories, custom instructions, and plugins to personalize responses. Before the conversation begins, you can turn personalization off. You can also save the chat later as a regular chat. Start a temporary chat Open a new chat. Select Temporary. Choose Personalized or Unpersonalized before you send your first message. Enter your message. Memory and personalization Before the conversation begins, choose how you want the temporary chat to respond: Unpersonalized: Does not use memory, custom instructions, or plugins. Personalized: Can use your existing memories, custom instructions, and plugins. Neither option creates or updates memories while the chat remains temporary. You cannot change personalization after the conversation starts. Your account and workspace restrictions take priority over the personalization choice for an individual temporary chat. ChatGPT may still use information from prior conversations for limited safety and security purposes. Turning off memory and personalization features does not disable safety features that may use limited, safety-relevant context in rare, high-risk situations to help ChatGPT respond more safely. For more information about safety context, see: Helping ChatGPT better recognize context in sensitive conversations. Save a temporary chat You can save a temporary chat to your chat history if you want to return to it later. Saving converts it into a regular chat, including when it started without personalization. From that point forward, the saved chat follows your account’s personalization and model-improvement settings. If Memory is on, the saved conversation may be used to personalize future responses. Account and workspace restrictions still apply. If you do not save a temporary chat, it will not appear in your history and cannot be reopened from history after you leave it. If your temporary chat includes uploaded files, eligible files may also be saved to Library when you save the chat, subject to availability and storage limits. For more information about saved files, see: Using Library to manage files in ChatGPT. Model training and retention A temporary chat is not used to improve OpenAI models while it remains temporary, whether or not you choose personalization. If you save it, your model-improvement setting applies. OpenAI may keep a copy of a temporary chat for up to 30 days for safety purposes. Once saved as a regular chat, the conversation follows the retention rules for saved chats. For more information about temporary chat retention, see: Chat and file retention in ChatGPT. Temporary chat compared with regular chats Temporary chat stays out of your history unless you save it. Regular chats stay in your chat history and follow your account’s personalization and data settings. Language English Login All Collections ChatGPT File uploads Chat and file retention in ChatGPT Chat and file retention in ChatGPT Understand how chats and files are retained and what happens when you archive or delete them. Updated: 6 days ago Overview Retention depends on the type of chat or file, the action you take, and any workspace retention policy that applies. Regular and archived chats remain saved to your account. Temporary chats follow different retention rules. Chats Regular and archived chats remain saved to your account until you delete them or an applicable workspace retention policy removes them. Archiving a chat does not change its retention period. When you delete a saved chat: ChatGPT removes it from your account view immediately. OpenAI schedules it for permanent deletion from its systems within 30 days, unless the chat was already de-identified and disassociated from your account or OpenAI must retain it longer for security or legal obligations. You cannot restore a deleted chat in ChatGPT or through OpenAI Support. For instructions to delete or archive chats, see: Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT. Temporary chat Temporary chats don’t appear in your chat history unless you save them. OpenAI may keep a copy for up to 30 days for safety purposes. If you save a temporary chat, it becomes a regular chat and follows the retention rules for saved chats. For more information about temporary chat, see: Temporary chat in ChatGPT. Files saved to Library When Library is available, files uploaded to or created in ChatGPT can be saved separately from the conversation that used them. Deleting a chat does not delete a file that remains saved in Library. Delete the file from Library when you also want to remove the saved Library copy. For Enterprise, Edu, and Healthcare workspaces, Library files follow the workspace retention policy. For more information about files in Library, see: Using Library to manage files in ChatGPT. Files in GPTs and projects Files uploaded as knowledge to a custom GPT are retained until the GPT is deleted. Files added to a project, including a shared project, are retained until the project or file is deleted or an applicable workspace retention policy removes them. After the GPT, project, or file is deleted, OpenAI schedules the associated data for permanent deletion within 30 days, unless security or legal obligations require longer retention. Enterprise compliance considerations Library files are available through Library-specific Compliance API endpoints while they are active. Files that are not saved to Library can expire separately from chat-retention settings. For Enterprise workspaces, some transient files can expire after 48 hours unless another retention period applies. After a Library file is deleted, expires under the workspace retention policy, or otherwise becomes inactive, it is no longer available through the active Compliance API endpoint. OpenAI backups may retain it for up to 30 additional days unless OpenAI must retain it longer for security or legal obligations. Language English Login All Collections ChatGPT Data Controls Data controls in ChatGPT Data controls in ChatGPT Learn how to manage model training, chat history, data exports, account deletion, and other privacy options in ChatGPT. Updated: 1 minute ago Overview Data controls let you choose whether your conversations help train OpenAI models. You can also use them to export data, manage shared links, and manage other data settings available to your account. Available controls depend on whether you are signed in, your plan, and your workspace settings. Find your data controls Open Settings and select Data controls to manage the options available to your account. The privacy center explains your privacy options and helps you find the settings that manage them. You can still manage those settings from the Settings menu in ChatGPT. Turn off Improve the model for everyone On personal workspaces with ChatGPT Free, Plus, or Pro, data sharing is enabled by default. You can turn off Improve the model for everyone and keep your chat history. When Improve the model for everyone is off, your new conversations won’t be used to train OpenAI models. They can still appear in your chat history. Voice mode has a separate control for sharing data for model training. The Privacy Portal has more information about your privacy options. Web (signed in) Open your account menu. Select Settings. Select Data controls. Select Improve the model for everyone, turn it off, and select Done. iOS and Android Open the sidebar. Select your profile icon to open Settings. Select Data controls. Turn off Improve the model for everyone. When you’re signed in, Improve the model for everyone applies to your account across web and mobile devices. Signed-out use When using ChatGPT without signing in, open Settings and turn off Improve the model for everyone. Your choice is saved in that browser. Manage model training for Codex tasks If you use Codex on a personal ChatGPT plan, Improve the model for everyone also applies to your Codex tasks. Turn it off in ChatGPT, or select Do not train on my content in the Privacy Portal. Either option is sufficient for ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks. You don’t need to do both. After you opt out, new conversations and tasks won’t be used to train OpenAI models. Codex has a separate Include environments setting in Codex settings that controls whether additional context from your Codex environments can be used to train OpenAI models. Changing your ChatGPT setting or opting out through the Privacy Portal does not change that setting. What happens to saved chats Turning off Improve the model for everyone does not delete or hide saved chats. Regular chats remain in history. Archived chats remain archived. When Locked chats is available for your account, locked chats remain saved but are hidden from the regular sidebar, Search, recents, notifications, and previews. Your Improve the model for everyone setting and applicable workspace policies continue to apply. Project chats remain in their projects. Archiving a chat does not change whether it can be used to train OpenAI models. Your Improve the model for everyone setting continues to apply. Temporary chat While they remain temporary, these chats: do not appear in your chat history do not create or update memories are not used to improve OpenAI models may be retained for up to 30 days for safety purposes Before the conversation begins, choose whether the temporary chat is personalized. A personalized temporary chat can use existing memories, custom instructions, and plugins, subject to your account and workspace settings. You can turn personalization off when you start the chat. If you save a temporary chat, it becomes a regular chat in your history and follows your account’s personalization and model-improvement settings. For more information about temporary chat, see: Temporary chat in ChatGPT. Export your data Signed-in ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users can request a copy of their data from ChatGPT settings. You can also use the OpenAI Privacy Portal. Self-service data export is not available for ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces and should be directed to the workspace owner. Eligible ChatGPT Edu workspaces may allow exports when an administrator enables them. For more data export instructions and availability, see: Exporting your ChatGPT history and data. Delete your account Signed-in ChatGPT Free, Go, Plus, and Pro users can delete their account from their ChatGPT settings. Deleting your OpenAI account is permanent and affects its associated data and subscriptions. Self-service account deletion is not available in managed workspaces and should be directed to the workspace owner. Contact your organization for help with a managed account. For account-deletion instructions and important billing information, see: Deleting your ChatGPT account. Memory and safety context Memory controls how ChatGPT personalizes responses. Model-training controls determine whether eligible content can be used to improve OpenAI models. These are separate settings. Turning off memory or personalization does not disable safety features that may use limited, safety-relevant context in rare, high-risk situations to help ChatGPT respond more safely. For more information about Memory, see: Memory in ChatGPT. Managed workspaces By default, OpenAI does not use content from ChatGPT Business, Enterprise, Edu, or ChatGPT for Healthcare workspaces to train its models. By default, OpenAI also does not use inputs and outputs provided through the API Platform to train its models. Managed workspaces can also have organization-level retention, Memory, compliance, and access settings. Your organization’s privacy, retention, and access policies still apply. For more information about enterprise privacy, see: Enterprise privacy at OpenAI. Ads controls Where ads are available, separate controls can determine how ads are personalized. For more information about ads, see: Ads in ChatGPT. FAQ Can I change the Improve the model for everyone setting? Yes, when the setting is available for your account. Parent or guardian settings and workspace controls may limit whether you can change it. Does turning off model training delete my chats? No. Delete a chat or your account separately when you want to remove content. What happens if I previously opted out through OpenAI Support or a privacy request? OpenAI continues to honor the opt-out associated with the account. What happens if I submit feedback after opting out of training? If you choose to provide feedback, such as selecting thumbs up or thumbs down on a response, the entire conversation associated with that feedback may be used to train OpenAI models. For more information, see: How your data is used to improve model performance. Need more help? Contact us for product questions, account support and general troubleshooting.
