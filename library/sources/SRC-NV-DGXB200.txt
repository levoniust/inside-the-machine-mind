##  NVIDIA Blackwell FAQs

##  How much does NVIDIA Blackwell inference cost per million tokens?

NVIDIA Blackwell-powered systems deliver AI inference at $0.02 per million tokens on GPT-OSS-120B using NVIDIA
TensorRT™-LLM, as verified by [SemiAnalysis InferenceX benchmarks ](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com/)(Q1
2026). $0.02 per million tokens on GPT-OSS-120B represents a 5x improvement over Blackwell’s own launch-day
costs, driven by continuous software optimization.

##  Is NVIDIA Blackwell more efficient than NVIDIA Hopper for inference cost per token?

The most important metric for AI inference TCO is the cost per token, or the price-performance actually
delivered. According to [SemiAnalysis InferenceX benchmarks](https://inferencex.semianalysis.com/) as of April
2026, NVIDIA Blackwell-powered systems deliver inference at approximately $0.02 per million tokens at 55
TPS/user for GPT-OSS-120B using TensorRT-LLM—roughly 4.5x cheaper than NVIDIA Hopper-powered systems at $0.09
per million tokens with vLLM.
