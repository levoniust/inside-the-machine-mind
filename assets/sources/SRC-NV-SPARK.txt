###  Overview

##  Your Desktop Agent Computer

Powered by the NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip, NVIDIA DGX Spark™ is a complete platform for local
autonomous agents. Large local memory, powerful AI compute and the NVIDIA AI software stack enable agents and
large models to run locally, reducing the need for cloud-based token generation resources. With a compact,
power-efficient design, DGX Spark is built to run always-on agent workloads—right from your desktop.

###  NVIDIA GB10 Superchip

###  Coherent Unified System Memory

Run AI development and testing workloads with state-of-the-art AI models at your desktop with a large, unified
system memory. Available in 64 GB* or 128 GB memory configuration options.

###  NVIDIA ConnectX Networking

High-performance NVIDIA ConnectX™ networking enables up to four DGX Spark systems to work together on larger
AI models, deliver faster inference, and run multi-agent workloads.

###  NVIDIA AI Software Stack

Utilize a full-stack solution for generative AI workloads, encompassing NVIDIA tools, frameworks, libraries,
and pre-trained models, including NVIDIA NIM™.

###  NVIDIA Sync

Connect to your DGX Spark from your laptop or desktop and monitor system status, launch Cursor, VS Code, or AI
Workbench remotely, and configure up to four devices into a high-speed multi-node cluster. Install the latest
software updates to get the most out of your system.

##  Workloads

##  Accelerate All Your AI Workloads

Delivering the power of Grace Blackwell in a desktop-friendly size, DGX Spark is ideal for AI developer,
researcher, and data scientist workloads.

####  Agentic AI

Build, run, and manage always-on AI agents from your desk.

DGX Spark provides a complete local platform to develop, test, and deploy autonomous AI agents. With the
NVIDIA AI software stack preinstalled—including tools for agent orchestration, evaluation, and secure local
deployment—developers can build agents that run persistently and privately, without cloud dependency. From
single-agent prototypes to multi-agent workflows, DGX Spark supports the full agentic development cycle.

####  Prototyping

Develop, test, and validate AI models and applications.

With the NVIDIA AI software stack, DGX Spark provides a platform for developers to create, test, and validate
AI models, and build AI agents, AI-augmented applications, and solutions. For final tuning or deployment,
conveniently evaluate the work for eventual migration to the NVIDIA DGX™ cloud or other NVIDIA-accelerated
data centers or cloud infrastructures.

####  Fine-Tuning

Fine-tune AI models up to 70 billion parameters.

Improve pre-trained model performance by fine-tuning on DGX Spark. With up to 128 GB of unified system memory,
fine-tune models up to 70 billion parameters to customize AI models and solutions for specific needs and use
cases.

####  Inference

Test, validate, and inference with AI models up to 200 billion parameters.

Fifth-generation Tensor Cores with support for FP4 deliver up to 1 petaFLOP of AI computing performance,
combined with up to 128 GB of system memory, accelerating inference of state-of-the-art AI models to test,
validate, and deploy from your DGX Spark.

####  Data Science

High-performance data science at your desk.

DGX Spark’s combination of up to 128 GB of unified memory and 1 petaFLOP of parallel throughput maximizes
performance of large, computationally complex data analytics and machine learning workflows at your desk.

####  Edge Applications

Develop edge applications with NVIDIA AI frameworks, including NVIDIA Isaac™, Metropolis, and many others.

DGX Spark offers an exceptional platform for developing robotics, smart city, and computer vision solutions.
NVIDIA frameworks include [Isaac](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac), [Metropolis](https://www.nvidia.com/en-
us/autonomous-machines/intelligent-video-analytics-platform/), and
[Holoscan](https://developer.nvidia.com/holoscan-sdk), enabling developers to leverage the power of DGX Spark
to develop edge applications quickly.

##  FAQ

###  What is the NVIDIA DGX Spark platform?

NVIDIA DGX Spark belongs to a new class of computers designed from the ground up to build and run AI. Powered
by the NVIDIA GB10 Grace Blackwell Superchip, it's a complete local AI platform for agents, LLM, image and
video, and multimodal inference, fine-tuning, data science, and edge development, bringing the power of Grace
Blackwell to every desk. Connect up to four systems via NVIDIA ConnectX-7 to scale to larger models and more
complex workflows

###  Who is the target user of DGX Spark?

NVIDIA DGX Spark is designed for AI developers, researchers, data scientists, and students who need powerful,
always-on AI compute on the desktop—without relying on cloud or data center access for every workload. Whether
working independently or as part of an enterprise fleet, DGX Spark users share a common need: large local
memory, the full NVIDIA AI software stack, and a development environment consistent with production
infrastructure.

###  What software and hardware support is available for DGX Spark?

DGX Spark ships with DGX OS and additional NVIDIA tools. DGX OS is a Linux distribution based on Ubuntu and
matches the Linux distribution running on other DGX platforms. DGX Spark comes pre-installed and/or supports a
large catalog of tools, frameworks, models, and software for development use from NVIDIA NGC. For example:

  1. NVIDIA NeMo framework for model fine-tuning
  2. NVIDIA RAPIDS libraries for accelerated data science
  3. NVIDIA Blueprints for building agentic AI applications
  4. NVIDIA NIM microservices for testing optimized inference results
  5. NVIDIA NemoClaw, an open-source reference stack that adds privacy and security to OpenClaw, with a single-command install of the OpenShell runtime, NVIDIA Agent Toolkit, and Nemotron models
  6. Common frameworks such as PyTorch, Python, and Jupyter notebooks

DGX Spark users can register free of charge for the NVIDIA Developer Program to get access to NGC including
additional SDKs, toolkits, trained models, and workflows.

Hardware support will be provided by the vendor that sold the system.

###  How many DGX Spark systems can I connect?

Up to 4 DGX Spark systems can be connected using NVIDIA ConnectX-7 networking. You can cluster two or three
systems with a single QSFP cable; clustering 4 systems requires an external network switch. For optimal load
balance and parallelism, connect DGX Spark in a power-of-2 number

Configuration  |  DGX Spark 64 GB*  |  DGX Spark 128 GB  |  Connection   
---|---|---|---  
1 × DGX Spark  |  64 GB  |  128 GB  |  –   
2 × DGX Spark  |  128 GB  |  256 GB  |  Direct QSFP cable, no switch required   
4 × DGX Spark  |  256 GB  |  512 GB  |  200 GbE switch (QSFP56-DD, RoCE v2)   
  
**Details on QSFP Cables and Switches**

Each DGX Spark has two QSFP ports on the back of the device. Each port provides up to 200 Gigabits per second
(Gb/s), but the incoming speed also depends on the cable you use. Approved cables are:

**Amphenol:** NJAAKK-N911 (QSFP to QSFP112, 32AWG, 400mm, LSZH), _NJAAKK0006 is the 0.5m version of this
cable_

**Luxshare:** LMTQF022-SD-R (QSFP112 400G DAC Cable, 400mm, 30AWG)

Minimum requirements for switch:

  * Minimum 4 ports QSFP56-DD
  * Breakout to 25/50/100/200/400 G
  * Recommended max port speed of 200G–400G per port
  * One 1/10GbE management Ethernet port
  * Supports RoCE v2
  * Switching capacity/throughput: minimum 0.8–1.6 Tbps

*64 GB memory configuration is available exclusively through participating OEM partners.

###  What enterprise management options are available for DGX Spark?

The enterprise manageability features developed for DGX Spark 128 GB can also be applied to DGX Spark 64 GB.
Full details are located at <https://docs.nvidia.com/dgx/dgx-spark/enterprise-manageability.html>
