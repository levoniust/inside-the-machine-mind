# OpenAI Stargate Abilene

Abilene, Texas, United States

OpenAI Stargate Abilene is an operational AI data center with AI chips owned by Oracle and used by OpenAI. It
hosts an estimated 509k H100-equivalents of AI compute, supported by 421 MW of IT power at a capital cost of
$15.9 B. In Q4 2026, we project it to rise to 1,019k H100e AI compute, 843 MW IT power and a cost of $31.9 B.
We estimate that it uses Nvidia B200 and Nvidia B300 chips.

The timeline of IT power and AI compute capacity is based on satellite imagery, our cooling equipment power
model, company disclosures, regulatory filings, and drone imagery.

Abilene is the first of several planned Stargate sites across the U.S. and globally, expected to host OpenAI’s
largest compute cluster.

Building 1 looks close to finishing construction, with chillers in place. OpenAI
[stated](https://openai.com/index/five-new-stargate-sites/) that "Oracle began delivering the first NVIDIA
GB200 racks in June." The "tenant improvement" work [is not
due](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/TABS/Search/Project/TABS2025000156) until September, however.

Sep 26, 2025

[Satellite explorer  ](/data/ai-data-centers/directory/openai-stargate-abilene/satellite-
explorer?date=2025-09-26)

Building 1 is fully operational around this date. The first phase was "live" on September 30 according a
Crusoe [announcement](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-flagship-abilene-data-center-
is-live). While the first phase consists of Building 1 and 2, the language doesn't outright confirm that both
buildings are fully operational. The announcement also stated that "Oracle began delivering the first NVIDIA
GB200 racks in June", and we think 3 months is enough time to ram to 50,000 GB200s in Building 1. The
satellite image also suggests that Building 1 is complete (shell and equipment done, many cars neatly parked)
while Building 2 is ongoing (trucks and construction equipment still present). The 200 MW
[substation](https://www.mortenson.com/projects/abilene-data-center-development) built in 2022 and expanded in
2024 is enough to power both buildings. To the right of Building 2, five gas turbines are in place with five
more turbines still under construction. Chillers are partly installed for Building 3. Roof is complete for
Building 4. The larger 1 GW substation is not complete yet.
