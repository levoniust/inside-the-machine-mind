# Inside the Machine Mind

An interactive Android app (Flutter) that takes you on a journey through how large language models are built, guided by **Bit**, a small robot, following one question: *"Why is the sky blue?"*

1. **Where the Mind Lives**: the hardware, from a hard drive to a 100,000-GPU datacenter
2. **How the Mind Grew**: training, step by step, in plain language
3. **The Mind Answers**: inference, from a tiny model to a frontier model, and what people do with it

🚧 Under construction. This app is being built by a team of AI models running on a homelab.
